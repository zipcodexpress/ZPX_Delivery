# 0006 — One customer identity for sending and receiving

Purpose: record the shipping/recipient model and local payment boundary.
Audience: owner, developers, reviewers. Status: Accepted.
Owner: Richard (product/provider choices). Reviewed: 2026-09-19.

A CUSTOMER can send and receive with the same account. RECIPIENT is a relationship
to a shipment, not a separately provisioned role or account type. The existing
synthetic RECIPIENT login is simply a second CUSTOMER for testing. DRIVER,
HUB_STAFF, HUB_SUPERVISOR, DISPATCHER and ADMIN remain assigned staff capabilities.

The customer workspace has Sending and Receiving lists. Receiving access requires
both current verified contacts to match the encrypted recipient snapshot, plus a
fresh, expiring, one-use email challenge bound to that shipment and authenticated
customer. Unknown/mismatched references receive neutral challenge responses. This
is conservative: differing recipient contacts require a future support workflow.
A claim grants shipment visibility, never a pickup or door credential. No account
search or automatic linking by knowing another person's email is introduced.

Operators use a separate server-scoped queue. ADMIN/DISPATCHER grants are restricted
by organization and any assigned site. Hub access requires an active hub membership,
a matching scoped role, current hub location AND that hub's custody. Drivers receive
no operations-wide permission; assigned route/scan workflows remain later work.

## Local shipping and payments

Synthetic active sites can be used for planning without unfreezing their doors or
claiming physical eligibility. Compartment dimensions are a fit check, not a
reservation. Quotes use the explicit DEMO-2026-01 test policy: 500 cents plus 100
cents per started kilogram, USD, 15-minute expiry. No live price or delivery promise
is implied. Existing synthetic drafts become explicitly development-only when
quoted. Non-synthetic/live rates fail closed until an operational policy is supplied.

LOCAL_TEST starts pending checkout against the sender's current shipment version
and saved quote. Its separate development/test confirmation action uses the saved
amount and rejects expired eligibility; it cannot charge a provider and refuses
production or non-test shipments. Paid test orders retain sender physical custody.
Confirmed test payment enables an authenticated, watermarked 4×6 PDF label with
stable SI and public QR identity; no contacts, pickup secret or authorization are
printed. Reprints preserve identity and are audited. Revoked labels fail closed;
replacement requires the future independent-evidence supervisor workflow.

Richard selected **Authorize.net**, the existing ZPX provider, on 2026-09-19.
The provider adapter is not implemented in this checkpoint. Before sandbox validation,
the owner must make a sandbox account/credentials available through secure local
configuration (not chat). Plan to use hosted payment entry and server confirmation;
a browser success page must never mark a real order paid. Webhook verification needs
the Signature Key and raw-body HMAC-SHA512 validation, followed by authoritative
transaction/amount/merchant/order checks and durable replay handling.

Official references: [Authorize.net API](https://developer.authorize.net/api/reference/index.html),
[webhook authentication and retries](https://developer.authorize.net/api/reference/features/webhooks.html),
[sandbox setup](https://developer.authorize.net/hello_world/common_setup_questions.html).
PDF and QR are generated by locked [FPDF](https://github.com/Setasign/FPDF) 1.9.0
and [BaconQrCode](https://github.com/Bacon/BaconQrCode) 3.1.1 dependencies.

## Consequences and remaining work

Migrations 006/007 are additive; no applied migration checksum or legacy data changes.
Shipment mutations serialize with durable idempotency, actor checks on retries,
version preconditions, and transactional audit/events/outbox. Request fingerprints
are keyed, including claim codes, to avoid exposing a brute-forceable code hash.

No production checkout, refund, pickup grant, origin deposit, driver scan, hub
receiving, label replacement or physical reservation is claimed. These retain their
P2.1/P3/P4/P5 gates. The current operator UI is inspection, not dispatch/receiving.
